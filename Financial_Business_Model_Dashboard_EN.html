Key Revenue Insights

Diversified Revenue: No single stream exceeds 49% in Year 1, reducing dependency risk. Platform becomes more balanced by Year 3 as premium features scale.

Unit Economics: Why This Model Works

High Gross Margins (86-91%): SaaS-like model with minimal COGS. Satellite data free (Copernicus). Payment processing 2%. Platform scales with farmer volume without proportional cost increase.
Fast CAC Payback (3 months organic): Average farmer pays 6,000 TL/year subscription + 30,500 TL across other services (expert, B2B, B2C). Total 21,900 TL annual revenue per farmer × 91% margin = 19,962 TL gross profit. Payback: 1,500 CAC ÷ ~5,500 monthly profit = 3 months.
Network Effects Unlock Value: More farmers → better data → better recommendations → higher retention → lower churn. By Year 3, 1,500 farmers generate 48.8M gross profit, supporting 6M OpEx and 42.8M operating profit (88% margin).

Critical Success Factors & Risk Mitigation

✓ Success Drivers
1. Expert Network Scale: Tier 2-3 experts earn 370K-2M TL/year. Network grows through platform success.
2. Sponsorship Leverage: Consortium partners cover CAC, reaching 150K farmers vs. 2K organic.
3. Diversified Revenue: No single stream >49% Y1. B2B Sourcing + B2C Traceability drive Y2-Y3 growth.
4. Unit Economics: 40:1 LTV/CAC organic, 86%+ gross margins enable profitable growth.
5. Data Moat: 1,500+ farmers × 5 crops × 90-day plans = defensible competitive advantage.
⚠ Key Risks & Mitigations
Churn Risk (>5% monthly): Mitigation: Expert network + daily discipline system increases engagement, reduces 3-month churn to <3%.
Sponsorship Delays: Mitigation: Platform standalone breakeven M9 Y1. Sponsorship accelerates but not required.
Satellite Data Dependency: Mitigation: Sentinel-2/1 free (ESA Copernicus). Fallback: Synthetic NDVI from weather + ground sensors.
Expert Supply Constraint: Mitigation: 5,000+ available agronomists in Turkey. Tier 1-3 commission structure incentivizes participation.
Regulatory Changes: Mitigation: Data localization via AWS Turkey region + full KVKK compliance from Day 1.
