## BÖLÜM 14: RİSK AZALTMA (Risk Mitigation)

### Risk #1: Farmer Adoption Resistance

**Risk:** Farmers don't want to use digital tools
**Likelihood:** Medium (initial)
**Impact:** High (core business model)

**Mitigation:**
1. Education: Dr. Koçak trainings (reduce skepticism)
2. Proof: 3-month trial with ROI guarantee
3. Support: 24/7 helpdesk + WhatsApp bot
4. Incentive: +5% price premium if using platform

**Contingency:** If <20% adoption in Year 1, pivot to B2B-first

---

### Risk #2: Competition from Larger Players

**Risk:** Established companies (John Deere, global startups) enter market
**Likelihood:** Medium (2027+)
**Impact:** High (margin pressure, market share)

**Mitigation:**
1. Network effects: 500+ farmers + 300+ experts (defensible)
2. Local advantage: Dr. Koçak ecosystem built-in
3. Speed: 3-year head start (moat builder)
4. Positioning: Small farmer focus (vs. big-farm competitors)

**Contingency:** If competitor enters, accelerate geographic expansion

---

### Risk #3: Technology Platform Failure

**Risk:** System downtime during critical seasons
**Likelihood:** Low (cloud infrastructure)
**Impact:** Very High (farmer trust loss)

**Mitigation:**
1. Infrastructure: AWS redundancy + multi-region
2. Monitoring: Real-time alerts, 24/7 ops team
3. Backup: Manual daily backup (WhatsApp available if down)
4. Insurance: Cyber liability + operational disruption coverage

**Contingency:** SLA 99.9% with refund for downtime

---

### Risk #4: Data Privacy/Security Breach

**Risk:** Farmer financial data compromised
**Likelihood:** Low (encryption implemented)
**Impact:** Very High (legal + trust)

**Mitigation:**
1. Encryption: AES-256, TLS 1.3
2. Compliance: KVKK, GDPR
3. Audit: Quarterly security audits (3rd party)
4. Insurance: Cyber liability 10M TL

**Contingency:** Incident response plan, credit monitoring for users

---

### Risk #5: Weather/Climate (Harvest Failure)

**Risk:** Bad weather reduces yield (not platform's fault, but affects economics)
**Likelihood:** Medium (climate variability)
**Impact:** High (farmer income, retention)

**Mitigation:**
1. Prediction: AI weather forecasting (72-hour alerts)
2. Risk management: Insurance partnerships (TARSIM)
3. Diversification: Multiple crops recommended
4. Contract: SLA excludes "act of god" scenarios

**Contingency:** B2B partnerships continue, B2C volume-based revenue declines

---

### Risk #6: Blockchain Regulation Uncertainty

**Risk:** Turkey/EU regulate blockchain usage in agriculture
**Likelihood:** Low (currently favorable)
**Impact:** Medium (traceability feature dependent)

**Mitigation:**
1. Compliance: Monitor regulatory landscape
2. Flexibility: Can turn off blockchain, still works as SaaS
3. Advocacy: Industry group engagement
4. Architecture: Hybrid model easier to adapt

**Contingency:** Operate as traditional SaaS if blockchain becomes restricted
