### 5.3 Proje Modeli

**Proje Örneği:**
- Title: "Mersin 20 ha Domates - Hassas Tarıma Geçiş"
- Duration: 6 ay

1. Technical Quality (5-point scale)

## BÖLÜM 8: BLOCKCHAIN STRATEGY

### 8.1 Hybrid Architecture (Why This Model?)

**Problem with All-Public Blockchain:**
- Slow (15 tx/sec)
- Expensive (every transaction costs $)
- Privacy: All data public (not good for farmer financial data)

**Problem with All-Private Blockchain:**
- Centralized (not decentralized, loses trust benefit)
- Can be changed (immutability compromised)

**Solution: Hybrid Model**
```
LAYER 1: HYPERLEDGER FABRIC (Private)
├─ Participants: Farmers, advisors, platform, gıda şirketi
├─ Speed: 3,500 tx/sec (fast!)
├─ Cost: 1M TL/year (reasonable)
├─ Privacy: Only participants see data (secure)
└─ Use: Daily operations (tasks, reports, transactions)

LAYER 2: ETHEREUM (Public)
├─ Participants: Global (anyone can verify)
├─ Weekly: Batch hash of 50 transactions
├─ Cost: 780K TL/year (public network)
├─ Purpose: Immutability anchor (can't alter private data)
└─ Use: Final proof (alıcı/tüketici doğrulama)
```

### 8.2 What Gets Recorded

**Daily on Hyperledger:**
- Task completion (photos, observations, timestamp)
- Lab test results (quality verification, digital signature)
- Advisor approvals (expert recommendation, digital signature)
- Transactions (payments, equipment rental)

**Weekly on Ethereum:**
- Batch hash (5-10 farms = 1 hash → Ethereum)
- Purpose: "Immutable proof on public blockchain"
- Cost: 5 USD/week (affordable)

**Consumer Verification:**
- Scan QR code
- Platform: "Doğrula blockchain'de"
- Ethereum: "Evet, bu hash real" (public proof)
- Consumer: "Güvenilir"

### 8.3 Smart Contracts

**Contract 1: PaymentExecutor**
```
IF Lab_Test = PASS
THEN Auto_Release_Payment_to_Farmer
  ├─ Farmer earns 30K TL (not 35K, after platform fee)
  └─ Timestamp: Immutable blockchain record
```

**Contract 2: TokenReward**
```
IF Farmer_Task = COMPLETED_ACCURATELY
THEN Award_1_TOKEN (= 1 TL value)
  ├─ Incentivize accurate reporting
  └─ Tradeable for platform services
```

### 8.4 Cost & Benefit

**Cost (Year 3):**
- Hyperledger infrastructure: 1M TL
- Ethereum anchoring: 780K TL
- Total: 1.78M TL

**Benefit:**
- Fraud prevention: -99% (tampering impossible)
- Consumer trust: +30% price premium
- B2B confidence: +15% supply chain compliance
- Total value: ~50M TL (fraud avoidance + premium)

**ROI: 28x**

## BÖLÜM 10: SUSTAINABILITY & ETHICS

### 10.1 "Optimum Benefit Not Maximum Profit"

**Philosophy:**
- Farmer makes money, BUT long-term
- Not: +200% fertilizer for +50% yield then soil dies
- But: +30% yield, soil healthy 30+ years

**Implementation:**
- Every recommendation: profit + sustainability balance
- Algorithm: Rejects maximum-profit if environmental risk > threshold
- Education: Explain why (long-term farming viability)

### 10.2 Environmental Impact Metrics

**Tracked (Blockchain):**
- Water usage (L/hectare)
- Fertilizer quantity (kg/hectare)
- Pesticide usage (L/hectare)
- Soil health score (+ or -)
- Carbon footprint (calculated)

**Sustainability Score (1-10):**
- Score 8+: "Excellent" (farmer profile)
- Score 5-7: "Good"  
- Score <5: "Improvement needed" + recommendations

**Market Advantage:**
- Retailer (Gıda şirketi) preferring 8+ score
- Consumer willing to pay +10% for 8+ score
- Incentivizes sustainable practices

### 10.3 ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance)

**Environmental:**
- Water efficiency tracking
- Soil health monitoring
- Pesticide minimization
- Carbon footprint calculation

**Social:**
- Fair farmer pricing (eliminate middleman)
- Expert income scaling (5000 advisors)
- Community support (forum, best practices)
- Knowledge dissemination (free content)

**Governance:**
- Transparent algorithms (explainable AI)
- Community oversight (ratings, reviews)
- Regular audits (third-party verification)
- Dispute resolution (fair arbitration)
