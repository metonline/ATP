### Issue #2: LTV/CAC Ratio (45:1 → 40:1)
**Status:** ✅ FIXED & CONTEXTUALIZED

**What was wrong:**
- Stated: 45:1 LTV/CAC
- Industry benchmark: 3.2:1 median (top quartile 5:1)
- Our 45:1 was **9x higher than benchmark**

**Fix Applied:**
- Recalculated using gross profit base (not revenue): 59.9K TL ÷ 1.5K CAC = **40:1**
- Added context: "Dr. Koçak network advantage enables low CAC"
- Noted sensitivity: "Assumes <10% monthly churn; 10%+ churn breaks model"
- Documented CAC as blended: 50% referral (500 TL) + 50% paid (3K TL)

**Reality Check:**
- 40:1 still high, but justifiable given:
  - Referral network (free/low-cost acquisition)
  - High gross margin (91%)
  - Sticky product (advisor integration)

**Impact:** Ratio more credible; investors understand the assumption structure

---

### Issue #3: CAC Realism (1.5K TL vs $300-$1200 USD benchmark)
**Status:** ✅ ADDRESSED

**What was wrong:**
- Stated: 1.5K TL (~$50 USD)
- Benchmark: $300-$1200 USD per customer
- Our CAC was **6-24x lower** than benchmark

**Fix Applied:**
- Added CAC breakdown by channel:
  - Referral (Dr. Koçak network): 500 TL
  - Paid channels: 3,000 TL
  - Blended: 1,500 TL (Year 1 only; improves as referral % grows)
- Noted: "As referral adoption grows, blended CAC declines to <1K"
- Sensitivity: If referral fails, CAC rises to 3K → model breaks

**Investment Implication:**
- **Success hinges on Dr. Koçak network activation**
- If referral strategy fails: Break-even delays from Month 9 to Month 14-15
- Mitigation: Co-invest in Dr. Koçak incentives early

**Impact:** CAC assumption realistic if referral assumption holds

- Impact: Year 1 farmer acquisition 50 vs 150 (3x difference)
- Dependency: **Product-market fit validation in pilot**
- Mitigation: NPS >50 target; build feedback loops early

**3. ADOPTION VELOCITY - MEDIUM RISK** 🟡
- Swing: 50 vs 150 farmers Year 1 = 2x farmer count difference
- Impact: Revenue impact 750K vs 3.9M (5x)
- Dependency: **Go-to-market effectiveness + Dr. Koçak network**
- Mitigation: Phase 2 (Beta) critical validation gate

**4. REVENUE per FARMER - LOWER RISK** 🟢
- Swing: 15K vs 26K TL = 1.7x difference
- Impact: Correlates with module adoption (tied to churn)
- Dependency: **Feature rollout execution**
- Mitigation: Modular revenue growth (subscription first, premium add-ons)
