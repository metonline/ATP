## SLIDE BREAKDOWN

### SECTION 1: CONTEXT & PROBLEM (Slides 1-6)

**Slide 1: Title Card**
- Logo + Platform name
- Tagline: "Professional Agriculture Powered by AI"
- Date + Presenter

**Slide 2: Turkey's Agricultural Opportunity**
- 9 million farmers
- 23 million hectares
- €60-80B annual production
- Global food security importance

**Slide 3: The Problem - Production Planning**
- 85% use intuition (no planning systems)
- ±25% yield variance year-to-year
- No cost tracking
- Unpredictable risk management
- **Impact:** -12.5% productivity = €150K lost income/farmer

**Slide 4: The Problem - Expert Access**
- 5,000+ qualified agronomists exist
- Unreachable (travel costs 4-5 hours/farm)
- <10% of farmers use paid advisory
- Knowledge locked away, underutilized

**Slide 5: The Problem - Consumer Trust**
- No farm-to-table traceability
- All products commodity-priced (25 TL/kg)
- Consumers willing to pay +20-30% for transparency
- Middlemen capture 40% of farmer income

**Slide 6: The Moment (Why Now?)**
- Post-COVID smartphone adoption: +40%
- Global ag-tech acceleration (precision farming trending)
- Blockchain mainstream adoption
- ESG/sustainability premium growing
- **Turkey ready for digital agriculture transformation**

## DESIGN NOTES

**Visual Consistency:**
- Color scheme: Green (agriculture) + Blue (technology) + White (clean)
- Icons: Custom agricultural symbols (tractor, satellite, leaf, expert, market)
- Charts: Simple, clear (avoid data overload)
- Photos: Real Turkish farmers, fields, expert meetings

**Narrative Flow:**
- Slides 1-6: "This is a big problem"
- Slides 7-14: "This is our solution"
- Slides 15-18: "The market is ours to win"
- Slides 19-24: "The unit economics prove it"
- Slides 25-28: "We can execute it"
- Slides 29-30: "We need this capital to do it"

**Timing:**
- 2 min per slide = 60 minutes total (but typical: 20 min + 10 min Q&A)
- Fast through problem/solution (slides 1-14: 12 min)
- Linger on financials & moat (slides 19-26: 8 min)
