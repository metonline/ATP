## 1. Consortium Co-Sponsorship Model Framework

### 1.1 Why Consortium vs. Single-Sponsor

**Single-Sponsor Model (Traditional):**
```
One bank sponsors platform
├─ Sponsorship fee: 1M TL/year
├─ Exclusivity: Bank gets featured placement
├─ Risk: If bank withdraws, major revenue loss
├─ Reach: Limited to that bank's network (~500K customers)
└─ Issue: Competitor banks excluded, won't participate
```

**Consortium Model (Recommended):**
```
3-5 banks co-sponsor platform
├─ Sponsorship fee: 1.5M TL total, shared equally = 300-500K per bank
├─ Rotation: Each bank featured monthly (Month 1: Bank A, Month 2: Bank B, etc.)
├─ Benefits shared: Co-marketing, farmer education, referral leads
├─ Risk diversified: If one bank leaves, others remain
├─ Reach: 3 banks × 500K customers = 1.5M reach (3x single-sponsor)
├─ Competition incentive: Banks compete on loan terms/rates → farmers win
└─ Sustainability: Banks cooperate because competitors also participate
```

**Consortium Advantages:**
✓ Lower per-sponsor cost (shared) → easier to sell  
✓ Higher total revenue (more sponsors) → more sustainable  
✓ Reduced risk (diversified) → more stable partnerships  
✓ Larger reach (additive networks) → faster penetration  
✓ Better value for farmers (competitive pressure) → higher adoption

## 11. Risk Mitigation & Partnership Governance

### 11.1 Risks of Consortium Model

```
RISK 1: Partner Withdrawal
├─ Impact: If Tarım Kredi withdraws, lose 50% of farmers acquired via banking
├─ Mitigation: Multi-year contracts (3 years for banking, 2 years others)
├─ Mitigation: Diversify partners (5 banks > 1 bank)
├─ Mitigation: Create switching cost (integrated services = hard to leave)
└─ Fallback: Organic acquisition scales post-Year 2 (not entirely dependent)

RISK 2: Conflicting Partner Incentives
├─ Example: Tarım Kredi wants farmers as credit customers, not platform users
├─ Mitigation: Align incentives (bank earns commission on loans facilitated via platform)
├─ Mitigation: Exclusive features (banking widget only on IA platform)
└─ Mitigation: Win-win metrics (farmer conversion = bank metrics + platform metrics)

RISK 3: Data Privacy & Regulatory Risk
├─ Impact: Partners want farmer financial data, KVKK compliance required
├─ Mitigation: Clear data agreements (anonymization, consent, contractual)
├─ Mitigation: Compliance officer oversight
├─ Mitigation: ISO 27001 certification (security confidence for partners)
└─ Fallback: Can operationalize without data sharing (feature isolation)

RISK 4: Channel Conflict
├─ Example: Ülker has own procurement team, doesn't like IA intermediation
├─ Mitigation: Position as "logistics/verification service" not intermediary
├─ Mitigation: Batch aggregation = real service (saves Ülker operational work)
└─ Fallback: Can white-label for company (they operate their own "farmer app")
```
