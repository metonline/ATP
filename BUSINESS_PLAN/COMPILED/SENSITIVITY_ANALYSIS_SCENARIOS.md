**Date:** August 2026
**Model:** 5-year financial projections with key variable sensitivity

---

## SCENARIO DEFINITIONS

### SCENARIO 1: WORST CASE
**Assumptions:**
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** 3,000 TL (paid channels dominate, no Dr. Koçak referral advantage)
- **Monthly Churn Rate:** 10% Year 1, 5% Year 2, 3% Year 3+
- **Adoption Rate:** 50 Y1 → 150 Y2 → 300 Y3 → 600 Y4 → 1000 Y5 (conservative, slow growth)
- **Revenue per Farmer:** 15,000 TL (lower premium feature adoption, discount pricing)
- **Fixed Costs:** 850K TL Year 1 (hiring pressures)

**Narrative:** Market adoption slower than expected; CAC higher due to paid channels; churn higher due to poor product-market fit

---

### SCENARIO 2: BASE CASE (Current Plan)
**Assumptions:**
- **CAC:** 1,500 TL (blended: 50% referral 500 TL + 50% paid 3K TL)
- **Monthly Churn Rate:** 5% Year 1, 3% Year 2, 2% Year 3+
- **Adoption Rate:** 100 Y1 → 300 Y2 → 500 Y3 → 1000 Y4 → 1500 Y5 (realistic, pilot + word-of-mouth)
- **Revenue per Farmer:** 21,900 TL (all modules, full premium adoption path)
- **Fixed Costs:** 800K TL Year 1

**Narrative:** Dr. Koçak advantage holds; market adoption as planned; standard unit economics

---

### SCENARIO 3: BEST CASE
**Assumptions:**
- **CAC:** 1,000 TL (high referral adoption, network effects kick in)
- **Monthly Churn Rate:** 3% Year 1, 1.5% Year 2, 1% Year 3+ (strong product-market fit, high satisfaction)
- **Adoption Rate:** 150 Y1 → 500 Y2 → 1000 Y3 → 2000 Y4 → 3000+ Y5 (viral adoption, network effects)
- **Revenue per Farmer:** 26,000 TL (all premium features adopted, higher B2C sales)
- **Fixed Costs:** 800K TL Year 1 (efficient scaling)

**Narrative:** Referral network effects dominate; strong product resonance; faster market expansion

## SENSITIVITY DRIVERS (RANKED BY IMPACT)

### 1. **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)** - HIGHEST IMPACT

**Sensitivity Range:** 1,000 TL (best) → 3,000 TL (worst)

Impact on Year 1:
- CAC @ 1K TL: Break-even Month 4-5, Op Profit +2.5M
- CAC @ 1.5K TL: Break-even Month 9, Op Profit +770K
- CAC @ 3K TL: Break-even Month 14-15, Op Loss -170K

**Mitigation:** Dr. Koçak referral network critical → if fails, model breaks

### 2. **Monthly Churn Rate** - HIGH IMPACT

**Sensitivity Range:** 1% (best) → 10% (worst)

Impact on Year 1:
- 1% churn: 150 farmers by EOY → Revenue +2.5M
- 5% churn: 100 farmers by EOY → Revenue 2.37M
- 10% churn: 50 farmers by EOY → Revenue 750K

**Mitigation:** Strong product-market fit (MVP + pilot validation) essential

### 3. **Adoption Rate** - MEDIUM IMPACT

**Sensitivity Range:** 50 farmers (worst) → 150 farmers (best) Year 1

Impact on Year 1:
- 50 farmers: Revenue 750K, breakeven Month 14+
- 100 farmers: Revenue 2.37M, breakeven Month 9 ✓
- 150 farmers: Revenue 3.9M, breakeven Month 4-5

**Mitigation:** Pilot success → Beta expansion → Early adoption phases are critical

### 4. **Revenue per Farmer** - LOWER IMPACT

**Sensitivity Range:** 15K TL (worst) → 26K TL (best)

Note: Revenue per farmer scales with module adoption, which correlates with churn.
- Lower adoption (features) → Higher churn (dissatisfaction)
- Higher adoption → Lower churn (stickiness)

**Mitigation:** Feature roadmap execution, not just pricing

## BREAK-EVEN SENSITIVITY MATRIX

### What breaks break-even in each scenario?

**Worst Case (+14 months delay):**
- Root cause: CAC doubled (1.5K → 3K) + churn doubled (5% → 10%)
- Compounds to 50 farmers instead of 100
- Remedy: Reduce CAC to 2K or improve churn to 7%

**Best Case (-5 months early):**
- Root cause: CAC halved (1.5K → 1K) + churn halved (5% → 3%)
- Compounds to 150 farmers instead of 100
- Upside: Requires both CAC AND churn improvements (unlikely alone)

**Base Case (Month 9):**
- Conservative regarding break-even timing
- Assumes 50-70 farmers needed for break-even
- Currently projecting 100 farmers by EOY → Healthy margin

## RISK IMPLICATIONS & RECOMMENDATIONS

### MUST-MONITOR METRICS (Monthly)

1. **CAC Actual vs Plan**
   - Target: ≤1.5K TL blended
   - Threshold: If hits 2.5K → Model at risk
   - Action: Increase referral spend if paid CAC exceeds 4K

2. **Monthly Churn Rate**
   - Target: ≤5% Year 1, ≤3% Year 2+
   - Threshold: If hits 8%+ → Break-even delayed
   - Action: Launch product improvements, support intensification

3. **Farmer Acquisition Velocity**
   - Target: 8-10 farmers/month Year 1
   - Threshold: If drops below 5/month → Adopt adjustment
   - Action: Accelerate partnerships, increase marketing

4. **Revenue per Farmer Trend**
   - Target: Trend toward 22K TL by Q4
   - Threshold: If stalls <20K → Module adoption weak
   - Action: Feature launch acceleration, training intensification
