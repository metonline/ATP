Communication Delivery Layer (Critical for Farmer Access)

WITHOUT reliable communication, information never reaches farmers. Multiple channels ensure delivery in all connectivity scenarios.

📱
Mobile Push (Primary)
4G/LTE Connected: Real-time WebSocket push | Alert: <30 seconds to farmer's phone
🔄
Offline Sync (Fallback)
No Connectivity: Local SQLite buffers recommendations | When Online: Bi-directional sync with cloud | Result: Farmer sees data within hours, not days
💬
SMS/USSD (Edge Case)
No App/Data: Critical alerts via SMS | No Phone Signal: USSD (dial *123) for basic info
🗣️
Voice & Broadcast
Cooperative bulletin boards | Community messaging | Expert hotline (1-hour response)
