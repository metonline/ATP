## MODULE 14: RISK MITIGATION

### Risk #1: Farmer Adoption Resistance
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** High (core business)

```
Mitigation Strategy:
1. Education: Dr. Koçak-led farmer training sessions
2. Proof: 3-month trial with ROI guarantee (not charged if <+10% benefit)
3. Support: 24/7 helpdesk + WhatsApp bot (not just app)
4. Incentive: +5% price premium if actively using platform

Contingency: If <20% adoption Year 1, pivot to B2B-first model
(food companies mandate platform use as supplier requirement)
```

### Risk #2: Competition from Large Players
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** High (market share / margins)

```
Mitigation Strategy:
1. Network effects moat: 500+ farmers + 300+ experts (Year 2-3)
2. Local advantage: Dr. Koçak ecosystem (vs. faceless startups)
3. Speed advantage: 3-year head start (reach profitability before competition)
4. Positioning: Small-farmer focus (competitors target big farms 500+ hectares)
5. Data moat: 2+ years of farm data (proprietary AI training)

Contingency: If competitor enters, accelerate B2B partnerships
(Food company contracts lock in customer base)
```

### Risk #3: Technology Platform Failure
**Likelihood:** Low | **Impact:** Very High (farmer trust)

```
Mitigation Strategy:
1. Infrastructure: AWS multi-region + auto-failover (99.9% SLA)
2. Monitoring: Real-time alerts + 24/7 ops team
3. Backup: Manual offline mode (WhatsApp available if system down)
4. Insurance: Cyber liability + operational disruption coverage

Contingency: SLA 99.9% with customer credit for downtime
```

### Risk #4: Data Privacy / Security Breach
**Likelihood:** Low | **Impact:** Very High (legal + trust)

```
Mitigation Strategy:
1. Encryption: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
2. Compliance: KVKK (Turkish GDPR), GDPR-ready
3. Audit: Quarterly security audits (3rd party penetration testing)
4. Insurance: Cyber liability 10M TL

Contingency: Incident response plan + farmer credit monitoring
```

### Risk #5: Weather / Climate (Harvest Failure)
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** High (farmer income, retention)

```
Mitigation Strategy:
1. Prediction: AI weather forecasting (72-hour alerts)
2. Insurance partnerships: TARSIM integration (crop insurance)
3. Diversification: Platform recommends multi-crop strategy
4. Contract: SLA excludes "acts of god" (force majeure clause)

Contingency: B2B revenue stream independent of harvest
(subscription revenue continues even if poor harvest)
```

### Risk #6: Blockchain Regulation Uncertainty
**Likelihood:** Low | **Impact:** Medium (traceability dependent)

```
Mitigation Strategy:
1. Compliance: Monitor regulatory landscape (EU, Turkey)
2. Flexibility: Blockchain is optional (platform works as pure SaaS)
3. Advocacy: Industry group engagement (AgTech association)
4. Architecture: Hybrid model easier to adapt (can disable Ethereum, keep Hyperledger)

Contingency: Operate as SaaS without blockchain if restricted
(Only loses +5-10% revenue from premium pricing)
```
